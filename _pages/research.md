## Work in progress

### The Value of Insider Selling: Trading Flexibility, Managerial Incentive Design and Innovation

I study whether restrictions on insider selling affect managerial incentive design by exploiting the SEC's 2022 amendments to Rule 10b5-1. I construct firm-level measures of pre-amendment insider-trading restrictions from corporate disclosures and use a difference-in-differences design to compare firms that were relatively less restrictive before the amendments with firms whose existing policies already incorporated many of the newly introduced restrictions.
